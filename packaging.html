Packaging

The SKUs booked on the Filling report and the packaging materials they are made of.

You do not have access to Packaging. Ask an admin for view rights on "Packaging".

An SKU is what the Filling report books in: package, litres per SKU and its bill of materials (materials per SKU). The expected material use of a filling session is count × bill of materials. Inactive SKUs are no longer offered but stay on old reports; an SKU that is booked can only be made inactive.
