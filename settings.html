Cellar plan

The order of the cellar plan that is generated for each recipe, and the timing values of cellar additions (the Timing dropdown of Additions Cellar in the recipe). Drag rows by the handle to change the order. Fixed parts come from the recipe data; a timing value puts one step per cellar addition with that timing at its place. The Tag is the action tag that ticks the step off in the fermentation log. "Processing" values are not in the plan: they show on the Processing tab. A change applies to a recipe when it is saved, or to all recipes at once with the button below, which also gives planned batches without log entries the new plan; batches that have started keep theirs.

Cellar action tags

Tags for the actions in the fermentation log. A milestone tag can tick off a step of the cellar plan: the n-th log entry with that tag ticks off the n-th plan step with that tag. Inactive tags are no longer offered but stay on old entries. A tag that is used can only be made inactive. Drag rows by the handle to reorder them.

Breweries

The breweries a recipe can belong to (Brewery on the recipe's General tab, also a column on the recipe overview). The order is the order of the dropdown. A brewery with recipes can only be made inactive: it is then no longer offered, but stays on its recipes.

Suppliers

The suppliers to choose from on the ingredient card (Supplier #1–#3) and on Book in material, and later on contracts. A change is saved as soon as you leave the field. A rename applies everywhere the supplier is used. A supplier that is used can only be made inactive: it is then no longer offered, but stays where it is used.
